# Unresponsive but breathing normally — recovery position

**Trigger:** branch from [[fall-no-response]] or [[faint-syncope]] — person does not respond, chest rises and falls normally.

## Steps

1. **Help is already called** (this skill is only reachable post-escalation). If somehow not: caregiver + {emergency_number} now.
2. **Coach the bystander into the recovery position** (protects the airway from tongue/vomit):
   1. "Kneel beside them. Straighten their legs."
   2. "Take the arm nearest you and place it at a right angle, palm up."
   3. "Bring the far arm across the chest, back of their hand against their near cheek — hold it there."
   4. "With your other hand, pull the far knee up so the foot is flat on the floor."
   5. "Pull that knee toward you to roll them onto their side, facing you."
   6. "Tilt the head back slightly so the airway stays open. Mouth pointing slightly down."
3. **Caveat for falls:** if the fall was violent (stairs, height) suspect spine injury — move them only as much as needed to keep them breathing. Airway beats spine.
4. **Monitor breathing continuously** until responders arrive: "Watch their chest. Tell me immediately if the breathing changes or stops." Breathing stops or becomes gasping → [[unresponsive-not-breathing-cpr]].
5. Every 2 minutes, re-check response: speak their name, check breathing is still normal.
6. Keep them warm (coat/blanket over them); do not give food or drink.

## Say this (Herald phrasing hints)

- The recovery position has 6 sub-steps — deliver strictly one at a time, confirm each ("done?") before the next. Never read the whole list.
- Time-stamp aloud for responders: "They have been unresponsive for four minutes, breathing normally."

## Escalate when (Guardian policy)

- Already escalated. Report state changes (breathing change, any response) to caregiver/responders immediately.
