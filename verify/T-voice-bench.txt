FINDING 1 - session-snapshot cancellation storm (the dropped-speech cause)
Node log, one session: listen generations 2, 3, 4, 6 ALL cancelled by
session snapshots / says; each ended "transcript discarded". Of ~7 session
listens opened in 2.5 min, exactly ONE survived to publish a heard (gen 5).
Gen 4 was killed by a snapshot with no say pending at all:
  23:00:26.328 session listen open (gen 4, timeout 15s)
  23:00:46.615 session snapshot 01KZCMTPT9... (state=active) cancelled listen gen 4
  23:00:47.576 session listen gen 4 cancelled - transcript discarded (0 chars)
Mechanism: engine append() republishes the full session doc on EVERY internal
log line (25 snapshots for one session); node on_session_event cancels the
in-flight listen on every arrival. Anything the person was saying at that
moment is discarded and never published. ("0 chars" is what the cancelled ASR
call returned, not proof nothing was being said - cancel throws the audio away.)

FINDING 2 - TTS dominates latency; chained says produce 6-15 s deaf stretches
  tts local-ecd33c84 (83 chars, safety): 9.57 s  -> deaf 10.07 s total
  tts local-2ac9570e (80 chars, comfort): 4.62 s -> deaf  5.12 s (chained: 15.2 s continuous)
  tts local-01310f63 (32 chars): 1.81 s; local-48d454e7 (55 chars): 3.12 s (chained: 6.0 s)
  tts local-61058c98 (71 chars): 3.94 s; local-3dced66a (80 chars): 5.75 s (chained: 10.7 s)
Roughly ~9 chars/s synth+playback. The engine often queues a safety reply AND
a comfort line back to back; the node speaks both, deaf throughout (half-duplex
by design), +500 ms guard per line.

FINDING 3 - one TTS runner hang cost the session its opening line + 30 s
  22:59:13.871 tts start local-177d4afb (45 chars, prio safety)
  ~30 s later: TTS ('Connection aborted.', RemoteDisconnected(...)) - never spoken
Matches the known audio-analytics runner failure mode
(docs/operations/troubleshooting.md). Self-recovered on the next say this time.

FINDING 4 - engine/LLM legs are NOT the bottleneck once warm
Session jsonl: classify warm 424-494 ms; reply wording 1 834 ms; comfort
1 496 ms; notify_contacts 1 038-1 252 ms; call_emergency 0 ms.
Cold first classify of the session: 5 545 ms (the 5.58 s heard->say outlier).
No "dispatch slow" (>1 s) warnings fired in this window.

FINDING 5 - wake gate rejections now visible (idle mode)
Ambient speech near the kitchen node is rejected every few seconds, e.g.
  22:57:45.338 wake gate rejected final (19 chars, window 19 chars)
Correct behavior (not addressed to us), but until today these drops were
completely invisible - a missed real wake would have looked identical to
silence. Now countable.

RANKED (for the fix round, NOT done in this pass)
1. Stop the snapshot cancellation storm: node should ignore snapshots that
   do not change session state, and/or engine should not republish the full
   doc per log line. Directly recovers most dropped speech.
2. Shorten spoken lines / avoid chaining safety+comfort back to back
   (deaf time scales with chars; caps already exist in llm.py).
3. TTS runner hang: watchdog/timeout around speak() so a hang costs seconds,
   not 30 s and a lost line.
4. LLM warm-up ping at session open (kills the 5.5 s cold classify).
