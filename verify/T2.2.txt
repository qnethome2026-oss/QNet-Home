=== the two design ambiguities resolved, and where ======================
1. on_enter_done is keyed by (phase, action), not action alone. §6's pseudocode
   says `a not in session.on_enter_done`; taken literally, call_help's
   notify_contacts is swallowed by escalate's and §7's second Telegram template
   ("calling emergency services now") can never be sent - contradicting §6's
   "up to three short pings per fall session". The (phase, action) key satisfies
   both: escalate -> check -> escalate never re-alarms (test_reentry_single_
   notification), and reaching call_help sends its own milestone once
   (test_silence_full_escalation asserts kinds ["escalate", "call_help"]).
2. Session `state` stays inside the wire contract's vocabulary (active/closed/
   cancelled, §4); the exit label that ended it ("ok", "resolved", ...) is kept
   on session.final and written as the closing phase line's "to" field. No
   contract change, and get_session_summary's inference off the last phase line
   keeps working.

=== also observed, worth knowing before the demo ========================
The phase timer is NOT reset when the person speaks (DESIGN §6: the timer starts
once, before the listen loop). An earlier live run at timer_scale 0.2 had the
6 s check timer fire while the person was still answering the pain question, and
the session escalated mid-answer. That is the specified behaviour and it errs
toward help - but at a demo it means the pain double-check has to be answered
inside the phase's 30 s, which is the real budget, not the scaled one.
