=== how the loop is scoped, and why (a design judgement) ================
DESIGN §6 names escalate and call_help by name. The engine picks them out of the
skill data instead of hardcoding them: the comfort loop runs in a phase of a
`safety` skill that fires on_enter actions - i.e. one where the engine has
already acted on the person's behalf. In fall.md that is exactly escalate and
call_help, and it is the right default for any future safety skill.
`comfort: {phases: [...]}` in house.yaml overrides it outright.
The interval is `comfort.interval_s` (default 20 s) and is scaled by
dev.timer_scale along with the phase timers.

=== what is NOT in this task ============================================
The line is assembled by the engine from logged facts, not worded by a model.
DESIGN §6 wants Gemma to word it ("give a brief, true update") with the facts
injected - that is T3.4, and it swaps only the sentence, not the schedule, the
drop rule, or the facts themselves.
