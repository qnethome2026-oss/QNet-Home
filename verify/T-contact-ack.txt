THE CHANGE
  The fall-response escalation Telegram became a QUESTION: the trusted contact
  gets 30 seconds to acknowledge before the (SIMULATED) emergency call fires.
  An ack ("ok" / "okay" / "on it" / "got it" / "omw" / "on my way" /
  "i got this" / "handling", case-insensitive, word-boundary) jumps the
  session to a NEW phase `contact_engaged`: the house tells the resident, by
  name, that the contact is coming; the comfort loop continues; a 180 s
  backstop still reaches call_help so an acked-then-silence never strands
  anyone. A contact reply "call 911" / "call emergency (services)" reaches
  call_help immediately, from escalate and from contact_engaged both. All
  contact matching is REGEX ON THE ENGINE RAILS - the LLM never sees or
  routes contact text (engine.CONTACT_ACK_RE / CONTACT_EMERGENCY_RE).

DESIGN HOLDS (DESIGN.md §6 invariants)
  * Timers and notifications stay engine-side; the question, the jump, the
    backstop and the confirmation all fire with no model in the loop
    (the live run below is --no-llm end to end).
  * contact_engaged is NOT an exit of escalate, so llm.options_for never
    offers it - the model cannot claim a contact engaged. The jump is returned
    by the engine's own listen loop, exactly like a timer goto.
  * on_enter stays once-per-(phase,action): re-engagement never re-sends the
    confirmation; re-escalation never re-alarms.
  * Cancel still wins from everywhere, including contact_engaged, and still
    sends the false-alarm follow-up.
  * The pain double-check is untouched; a person's reply in contact_engaged
    routes back to check like escalate's always did.
