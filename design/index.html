Smart Home Solution · ⏱ 2 min 30

A house that notices, answers, and acts.

Two rooms watch and listen entirely on-device. It acts on what it sees — a fall gets a spoken check-in, a contact at 30 s, help at 60 — and answers what you ask: "where are my glasses?" is checked by every room's own camera. No video or audio ever leaves the room.

Monitor, and act

Most monitoring stops at the notification — a camera sees something, a phone buzzes, and a human somewhere has to notice, interpret, and decide. That gap is where the minutes go. QNet Home closes it, and it works in both directions: the house can start a session because a model saw something, or you can start one by asking.
