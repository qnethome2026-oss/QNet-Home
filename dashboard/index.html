Help


    
What is QNet Home

    

      QNet Home watches over the rooms you put it in, so someone who lives alone
      is never alone after a fall. Each room understands what it sees and hears
      on its own, right there in the room — no video, no audio and no recording
      ever leaves the house. The only things that travel between rooms are short
      text messages, like “a fall was detected” or “the glasses are on the
      nightstand”.
    


    
What the statuses mean

    
    
      
  • All quiet — nothing is happening in that room.
    • 
      
  • Checking in… — QNet saw something and is asking whether the person is okay.
    • 
      
  • Needs attention — nobody answered, so QNet has started getting help.
    • 
      
  • Help is on the way — emergency help has been called.
    • 
      
  • All clear — the room settled down again a few minutes ago.
    • 
    


    
What happens when a fall is detected

    
    
      
  1. The room sees the fall and QNet speaks up straight away: “I saw you fall. Are you okay?”
    2. 
      
  2. It listens. If the person says they are fine, or that it was a false alarm, it stops there.
    3. 
      
  3. If nobody answers, QNet messages the trusted contacts on your list and keeps talking to the person so they are not alone.
    4. 
      
  4. If there is still no answer, QNet calls emergency services and says where the fall happened.
    5. 
      
  5. It stays with the person, telling them what has been done, until help arrives.
    6. 
    


    
How to cancel

    

      Say “false alarm”, “never mind” or “stop” out loud, in the room.
      QNet stops immediately and marks it as a false alarm. You can also press
      Mark as resolved on this screen while an alert is showing.
    


    
For first responders

    

      Say “I'm the first responder, what happened?” anywhere in the house and
      QNet will speak a short summary out loud: what it saw, when, what the person
      said, and who has already been contacted. It works at any point, including
      while an emergency is still in progress.
