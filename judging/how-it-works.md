# Snapdragon Multiverse Hackathon — How It Works

*(Transcribed from Image_20260803_114340_868.webp — Hackathon SharePoint Home page)*

Step into the Snapdragon Multiverse, Qualcomm's flagship external hackathon, now reimagined as an internal experience, giving employees the opportunity to collaborate, build, and innovate across the Snapdragon ecosystem. Bringing together teams from across the company, this event is designed to drive the next generation of multi-device AI experiences across compute, mobile, IoT, and cloud.

Each team will have access to a Copilot+ PC powered by Snapdragon® X Series processors as the central control hub, along with a mobile device, Arduino UNO Q, and Qualcomm AI Cloud 100.

Over five days, selected teams of 3–5 employees will build real-world applications that orchestrate intelligence across devices; from AI PCs and smartphones to microcontrollers and cloud infrastructure, addressing meaningful challenges aligned with Qualcomm's technologies, products, and strategic priorities.

Unlike traditional hackathons, there are no predefined tracks, teams are free to explore any use case, as long as it results in a meaningful, real-world application. Whether you're building productivity tools, intelligent assistants, system-level innovations, or entirely new multi-device experiences, the focus is on how systems work together, not just what runs on a single device.
